Moats Need Models NEW↗
TLDR AI (email)·email · today

A widely-circulated essay argues that defensibility in AI products comes from owning the full feedback loop — model, harness, workflow, and evaluation — rather than renting frontier capability from third parties. Reading between the linesThe 'moat comes from the loop' argument is the business-case version of what Anthropic and OpenAI have been building toward architecturally: if you rent their frontier model, they own your feedback loop.
